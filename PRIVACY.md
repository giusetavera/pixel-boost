# PixelBoost — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

PixelBoost is a plugin for ChatGPT and Codex developed by **Giuseppe Tavera** ([giuseppetavera.it](https://giuseppetavera.it)). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## The short version

PixelBoost does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data. The developer never sees your images, your messages or anything about you.

## How PixelBoost works

PixelBoost is made only of instructions (a "skill") that tell ChatGPT how to enhance the images you upload. It has no server, no database, no analytics and no connection to any third-party service. Everything happens inside ChatGPT.

## Your images and messages

The images you upload and the messages you write are processed by **OpenAI**, through ChatGPT's own image editing, exactly as they would be without the plugin. How OpenAI handles that data is governed by OpenAI's policies, not by this one:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use)

Your ChatGPT settings (for example chat history and data controls) apply as usual.

## Data the developer receives

None. The only exception is information you choose to send yourself, for example by opening an issue on GitHub or writing an email. GitHub issues are public: don't include personal data or private images in them.

## Children

PixelBoost is subject to the same age requirements as ChatGPT.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
