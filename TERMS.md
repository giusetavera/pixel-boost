## What PixelBoost is

PixelBoost is a free plugin that instructs ChatGPT's native image editing to make photos sharper and higher resolution while preserving the original. It runs entirely inside ChatGPT and has no servers of its own.

## Results are enhancements, not originals

PixelBoost uses a generative model. On degraded images the model may reconstruct detail that wasn't in the source, and a reconstruction can be plausible yet wrong. The "16K" reference in the prompt is a quality target, not a guarantee of output dimensions. Don't present the results as authentic originals where authenticity matters, such as documents, evidence, historical archives or forensic work.
